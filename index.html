Last updated: October 30, 2026

Seçilmiş elanlarda qeydlər is a browser extension that allows users to add private notes to their favorite marketplace listings and browse listing photos without opening individual listings.

The extension supports Turbo.az, Tap.az, Bina.az, and Boss.az.

Information the extension handles

  • Personal notes: Text that users choose to write for individual listings.
  • Listing references: Listing identifiers or URLs used to associate notes with the correct listings.
  • Page information: Relevant listing content and photo references read from supported marketplace pages to display notes and enable photo browsing.

Where information is stored

Personal notes and related listing references are stored locally in the user's browser using the Chrome extension storage API.

The extension does not operate its own account system or backend server for storing this information.

Removing the extension or clearing its stored data may permanently remove locally saved notes.

How information is used

The information handled by the extension is used only to:

  • Associate personal notes with the correct listings.
  • Display saved notes on supported marketplace pages.
  • Allow users to browse listing photos without opening individual listings.

The extension does not use this information for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or unrelated purposes.

Sharing and sale

The extension does not sell users' personal notes or related listing references, and does not transmit this information to the extension developer.

The extension reads relevant information from supported marketplace pages in the user's browser to provide its features. The supported marketplace websites remain subject to their own privacy policies and practices.

Permissions

Storage permission: Used to save users' personal notes and related listing references locally in the browser.

Host permissions: Required to read relevant listing information, display personal notes, and enable photo browsing on supported marketplace websites.

Data retention and control

Users can edit or delete their personal notes through the extension.

Locally stored information remains in the browser until it is changed or removed by the user, or until the extension's stored data is cleared or the extension is uninstalled.

Children's privacy

The extension is a general-purpose browser utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
